\# Vera Video Privacy Policy



\*\*Effective date:\*\* August 5, 2026



\## Summary



Vera Video collects \*\*no user information of any sort\*\* and contains \*\*no ads\*\*.



\## Who this app is for



Music fans interested in videos of performances related to the Vera Project.

Vera Video is a collection of videos filmed at the Vera Project and the Black

Lodge venues. The app allows you to watch the videos using the youtube player,

search them, and make play lists with them.



\## What the app does



A back end list of videos is (hopefully) kept as up to date as we can keep it.

When the app loads it gets the current list of videos updated. The user can

search and create and add to play lists. Playback is by the youtube app which 

has a link to  open the regular youtube interface which can cast. The embedded

playback does not cast, so I don't think playlists work for casting.

\## Children



Simply assorted youtube videos, useful for children interested in the Vera Project

and live music in general. Not targeted at children particularly.

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future version of the app adds

a feature that handles data differently), the updated policy will be posted at
